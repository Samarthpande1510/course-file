    Privacy

    Student marks are sensitive, so this tool is built to keep them on your computer.

    • Your workbook is read on your deviceThe file is opened and processed inside your browser. It is never uploaded to a server.
    • Nothing is savedThe page keeps your data in memory only while the tab is open. Close the tab and it is gone.
    • No accounts, cookies or analyticsYou do not sign in, and this page sets no cookies and runs no tracking.
    • What third parties seeFonts load from Google Fonts, and the website host may keep standard request logs such as your IP address and the page you opened. Your workbook and student data are never part of those requests.
